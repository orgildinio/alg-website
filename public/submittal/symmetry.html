One post-top family. Three housing classes. Two optic platforms. 25 main-matrix SKUs. Symmetry replaces a stock-room of single-spec post-tops with one configurable spec line — 90 / 130 / 160W housings, 120-277V or 277-480V drivers, Black or Dark Bronze (White on the lone 130/27V/Frosted variant), and either the BeamLogic™ directional optic or the Frosted Optics symmetric optic. Field-tunable CCT via 3-CCTselect dip-switch (3000K / 4000K / 5000K) on every SKU. 4 SKUs are QuickShip (all LPTP130 + 120-277V combinations); the other 21 main-matrix SKUs are MTO with ~4-6 wk lead.
