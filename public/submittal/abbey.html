One roadway head. Photocell-ready out of the box. Abbey ships with the 7-pin ANSI C136.41 twist-lock receptacle standard — drop any vendor-neutral photocell or NEMA-DIM controller into the top without rewiring the head. Abbey-II adds the 4-step WATTSELECT dip-switch across 3 housing classes (100W / 200W / 300W) so one stocked SKU covers four wattage bins. 10-year limited warranty.
