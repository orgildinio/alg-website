The only true single-pin LED replacement for fluorescent T10 slimline sign lamps. Most "T10 LED replacements" are T8 lamps with adapter sleeves or R17d/HO double-ended lamps for legacy fixtures. LY-T10 ships the actual slimline T10 single-pin form factor that drops directly into the historical sign-fixture socket — no adapter sleeves, no ballast bypass, no housing modification. Zero mercury (the fluorescent it replaces does), RoHS, ETL US/C cross-border listed for US + Canada DOT projects. CLEARANCE INVENTORY — stock-on-hand defines availability; no re-production is planned.
