One family. Three authoritative SKUs. LPAR is the EVP confirm-beacon lamp built specifically for emergency-vehicle pre-emption signal heads (Opticom, STROBECOM, Federal Signal). Direct screw-in to existing E26 PAR38 sockets — no controller rewire. CRI 93, FCC Part 15 compliant for RF-dense cabinets, dielectric grease on the base for socket longevity. Black-Body 900lm variant MDOT-listed — the only PAR38 LED on the Michigan QPL. 3-year / 25,000-hour limited warranty.
