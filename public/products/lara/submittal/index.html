2 / 4 / 8 ft modular joiner system · directⒶIM up/down ratio · 3-WATTselect × 5-CCTselect · 0-10V dim to 1% · DampGuard IP40 · Made in USA
LARA is the planoⒶRCH ECO architectural linear pendant for office, healthcare, retail, foodservice, hospitality, and education ceilings — specified as a single SKU and field-tuned at install for both watt ladder (3-WATTselect DIP) and color temperature (5-CCTselect DIP 2700K-5000K). The directⒶIM mechanical control lets the installer dial the up/down ratio in the field, so a single fixture spec covers indirect, semi-direct, and direct ceiling washes without forking the catalog. The continuⒶRUN joiner system threads 2 ft / 4 ft / 8 ft modules into straight runs, L-corners (90°), T-branches (3-way), and X-crosses (4-way) for layout-driven continuous rows. 0-10V dimming to 1%, L90 @ 50,000 hrs, DampGuard IP40, Made in USA, and a 5-year limited liability warranty round out the package.
