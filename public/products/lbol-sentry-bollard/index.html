Highlights & Features

One bollard family. Twelve aesthetic combinations. Field-tunable in under 30 seconds.

Seven highlights collapsing a stockroom of single-spec bollards into one SKU — 12-combo crown × optic matrix, 4-WATTSELECT × 3-CCTSELECT field-tunability, factory TAA, and optional 90-min emergency battery.

— Hero Highlight

12 Architectural Combinations.

Three crown profiles (Dome · Round · Square) × four optic styles (Clear Louver · Clear Reflector · Frosted Louver · Frosted Reflector). One SKU family covers every architectural aesthetic from corporate campus to restaurant patio. Spec it once, dial it in on-site. Uncontested in the surveyed peer set — no other commercial bollard ships this matrix.

Field-Adjustable

4-WATTselect × 3-CCTselect.

One SKU, twelve in-the-box settings. Wattage (10/16/21/26W) and color temperature (3000K/4000K/5000K) both field-adjustable on a dual dip-switch in under 30 seconds. No SKU swap if the spec changes mid-project — change the dip-switch on-site and re-energize.

Code-Required Egress

EM-Ready · 90 Minutes.

Optional integrated emergency battery delivers 8W / 900 lm for 90 minutes when mains drop. No external EM driver, no separate junction box, no second SKU to stock — order the EM-equipped variant for life-safety paths, ADA routes, parking-lot corners, and any AHJ-required egress run.

Federal-Ready

TAA Compliant · Standard.

TAA compliance ships standard on every Sentry — no opt-in, no surcharge, no engineering hold. Critical for federal RFQs, GSA schedule, military base contracts, and any project covered by the Trade Agreements Act. Most architectural-bollard peers offer TAA only on request.

Durability

wetⒶGUARD · IP65 + 6kV Surge.

Heavy-duty die-cast aluminum housing with waterproof powder-coat finish. IP65-rated against dust + moisture. Dual-mode 6kV surge protection on both L/N-PE and L-N circuits standard. -4°F to 122°F operating range. Anti-UV polycarbonate lens won't yellow.

Two Years Above Industry

7-Year · 75,000-Hour Warranty.

Industry-standard architectural bollard warranty is 5 years. Sentry's 7 years is two extra years on every fixture you spec — a procurement-team line item that pays back across a 50-fixture campus. Anchored to 75,000 hours of L70 operation under standard ambient conditions.

In the Box

quickⒶDAPT Install Kit.

Every Sentry ships with the full install kit: bollard base flange, 4× anchor bolts, 4× M8-SS nuts, 4× spring washers, 4× round screw washers. No separate hardware order, no on-site improvisation, no “wait for the kit to arrive” truck roll. Pour the pier, drop the bolts, set the bollard.

constⒶNT-Ready

Emergency-Ready when the mains drop.

The Sentry's optional integrated emergency battery delivers 8W / 900 lm for 90 minutes of full pedestrian-area illumination when utility power fails — code-required egress for ADA routes, parking-lot corners, and any AHJ-required life-safety run. No external EM driver, no separate junction box, no second SKU to stock. The EM kit lives inside the same shaft as the standard driver, with a thermally isolated compartment to keep battery cycling clean.

Factory-installed integrated battery + emergency driver. Switches over instantly when mains drop — no relay click, no perceptible delay. 90 minutes at 8W / 900 lm output (sufficient illuminance for code-required pedestrian egress at typical bollard spacing). Self-test capability per UL 924. Operating ambient for EM mode: 50°F to 104°F.
