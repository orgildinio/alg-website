Highlights & Features

Spectra wins the high-output spec-grade brief.

Seven highlights — up to 6,180 lm @ 60W, 120-347V Universal voltage, Twist-Lock Modular with Separable J-Box, Tri-Mode Dimming, 27 configurations from 3 SKUs, Integrated J-Box + DampGuard™, and the full contrⒶLS by ⒶCS 4-device ecosystem.

— Hero Highlight

6,180 lm @ 60W. The high-output spec-grade play.

DMS3060 hits 6,180 lm at 60W — the highest output in the LCDL family. 37% above competing brands SLD8 (~4,500 lm max). Matches competing brands Gotham Incito at $$ spec-grade pricing (Gotham is $$$$ premium-tier). Only volume-tier LDN8's 8,000 lm tier exceeds Spectra in the spec-grade integrated-J-Box category — and volume-tier LDN8 is fixed-wattage, no Tri-Mode dimming flexibility. For projects spec'ing 5,000-6,000 lm per fixture in warehouse-adjacent · parking-deck · atrium · high-ceiling commercial spaces — Spectra is the best $/lm option in the spec-grade category. Astra's DMS3050 tops out at 50W / 4,650 lm; Spectra extends LCDL's reach into output ranges Astra can't cover.

Universal Voltage

120-347V Universal. Canadian commercial covered.

DMS1020 + DMS2740 ship 120-347V Universal voltage — covering US residential 120V, light-commercial 240V, US commercial 277V, and Canadian commercial 347V circuits. premium-tier downlights/SLD8 caps at 120-277V standard; competing brands's 347V variant is Made-to-Order at premium. premium-tier downlights/LDN8 same. competing brands Gotham Incito same. Spectra ships 347V from QuickShip stock. For Canadian projects + US industrial 480/277/347V transformer-secondary applications, Spectra is the single-source-of-truth advantage. (DMS3060 high-output 60W tier is 120-277V — power constraint at 60W exceeds 347V driver bandwidth.)

Twist-Lock Modular

Engine + Trim ship separately. Combined at install.

Twist-Lock Modular Design — engine module twists and locks securely into trim. The separable J-Box and trim provide distributor-friendly inventory optimization · stock fewer base drivers · combine with finish at install. competing brands SLD's HOMER housing and volume-tier LDN's bonded driver-trim are fixed-architecture · one driver = one SKU per finish per size. Spectra splits driver-module + trim into 2 stocking SKUs per configuration · half the SKU count for the same configuration depth. Tool-free snap-in installation · seamless integration of multiple trim options · easy field adjustments for re-spec without RMA.

Triple Threat

Tri-Mode Dimming. One SKU.

TRIAC + ELV + 0-10V from a single DMS1020 or DMS2740 fixture. Smooth, flicker-free dimming on every protocol. Named-dimmer compatibility — premium-tier wireless lighting control Diva DVSTV · premium-tier wireless lighting control MACL-153MH · Leviton IP-710-LFZ · Leviton DVCL-153PR · Legrand RH4FBL3PW. premium-tier downlights/LDN8 splits 0-10V vs ELV into separate SKUs. competing brands SLD ships 0-10V standard with ELV/TRIAC at premium. Spectra handles whatever the GC pre-wired without a return trip to the supply house. (DMS3060 high-output 60W = 0-10V dimming only · per datasheet "Key Specifications" · power constraint at 60W exceeds typical TRIAC/ELV dimmer bandwidth.)

Spec-Grade Install Envelope

Integrated J-Box + DampGuard + Twist-Lock.

Three install-speed features standard on every Spectra SKU. Integrated J-Box Design eliminates the need for traditional recessed cans — mounts directly into the ceiling for quick contractor-friendly installation, reducing job-site costs on both new construction and retrofit applications. DampGuard™ UL Damp Location rated for moisture-prone interiors (corridors · lobbies · indoor spaces with moderate humidity · NOT wet location like Astra's IP65). Twist-Lock Modular Design with separable engine + trim + reflector for inventory optimization. Light-weight aluminum die-cast housing for superior heat dissipation · deeply regressed matte diffuser conceals LEDs and reduces glare · spring clips · 5-year limited warranty.
