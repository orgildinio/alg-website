One disc-housing family. 8-step WATTSELECT driver. 165° optical rotation. OmniMax-I is the cityⒶRCH high-mast crown — a single die-cast disc-form housing that ships in 500W or 750W driver classes and rotates the optical assembly 165° for asymmetric throw correction. Specification grade: cULus + DLC Listed + IP65 wet location + 24/7-rated continuous operation. Up to four fixtures crown a single pole, each independently rotated for the field-coverage pattern. Silver 750W-120/277V is currently the only in-stock variant the other 9 SKUs are MTO with ~4-6 wk lead.
