Pick the beam. Spec one fixture.

Highlights & Features

Trackstar wins the volume-spec brief.

Seven highlights — three premium properties bundled in one SKU: 5-CCTselect, 20°-50° beamⒶDJUST, CRI 90+ truⒶCOLOR. trackⒶDAPT H + J track. 350° × 90° gimbal. DampGuard™. 60 install configurations from 4 catalog lines.

— Bid-Sheet Hammer

5-CCTselect × 20°-50° beamⒶDJUST. One SKU.

The unique-positioning gap. No competitor in the volume-spec window bundles 5-CCTselect, adjustable beam, and CRI 90+ in one SKU. Juno Trac-Master forks single-CCT + fixed beam per part number. competing brands Stasis is 2-CCT (3000K + 4000K) + 3 fixed beams. category competitors splits the three properties across separate families. Tech Lighting Element bundles all three at $$$ premium pricing. Trackstar bundles all three at the $$ spec-grade tier — no upcharge for color quality, no upcharge for adjustable beam.

Color Quality

CRI 90+ truⒶCOLOR. Standard at every CCT.

Juno Trac-Master, competing brands Stasis, and category competitors ship CRI 80 at the volume-spec tier — CRI 90 is a price-up SKU on each. Trackstar ships CRI 90+ R9>0 standard with 3-step MacAdam binning across all 5 CCTs. For boutique retail, jewelry, cosmetics, fine-art display, and curated hospitality where merchandise color drives sales, the procurement officer no longer chooses between color quality and budget.

SKU-Fork Collapse

trackⒶDAPT. H + J from one head SKU.

competing brands Stasis fits H-track only. Juno Trac-Master fits J-track only. category competitors ships separate part numbers per track standard (W / H / J / L). Trackstar's interchangeable trackⒶDAPT base mounts to H-Type (Halo H-track 1-circuit) and J-Type (Juno Trac-Master 1-circuit) infrastructure from a single catalog SKU. Mixed-standard installs — boutique retail rebuilds, hospitality renovations, gallery refreshes — collapse the bid sheet.

Aim Envelope

350° × 90° gimbal. 20°-50° zoom.

Three independent aim axes on every Trackstar — 350° horizontal rotation, 90° vertical pivot, 20°-50° beamⒶDJUST optical-collar zoom. Locking detent at every axis. Re-aim on seasonal merchandise rotation cycles without re-specifying the fixture, returning the head to the supply house, or re-trimming the install. Full-hemisphere accent coverage from one track location.

Damp Location

DampGuard™. Cooler-case ready.

Trackstar is UL Damp Location rated standard — opens cooler-case retail, produce-display merchandising, wash-down-adjacent hospitality, and humidity-prone gallery installations at the catalog price. Juno Trac-Master, competing brands Stasis, and Tech Lighting Element are dry-location only at the spec-grade tier. Application footprint expands without a price-up SKU and without sourcing a separate vapor-tight family.

Wattage Coverage

6 watt positions. 2 SKU classes.

LTRK3W20 covers 10W / 15W / 20W at 900-2,000 lm. LTRK3W30 covers 20W / 25W / 30W at 2,000-2,800 lm. Six selectable watt positions across the two-class catalog from a single fixture body — the 20W shared position hits peak efficacy at 100 LM/W on both classes. 3-WATTselect dip-switch on the head. The bid sheet picks the class; the install picks the position.

Spec Consolidation

60 install configs. 4 base SKUs.

2 classes × 2 finishes × 3 watts × 5 CCTs = 60 install configurations from a 4-line catalog spec. Plus continuous 20°-50° beamⒶDJUST adding an effectively infinite beam-angle dimension. Inventory carries 4 SKUs at the distributor instead of 60 part numbers. Field re-spec is a dip-switch flip + optical-collar rotation, not a return-and-restock. The architectural specifier's leverage on the bid sheet.

trackⒶDAPT

Two mounts. Two standards. Seven connectors.

trackⒶDAPT is the system underneath the head — Surface-mount (TSM) for retrofit drop-in, Embedded (TEM) for flush integration in new architectural ceilings, with the same head fitting both H-Type and J-Type 1-circuit infrastructure. Seven connector layouts cover any track geometry.

Surface-mounted track segment. Drops onto existing junction-box infrastructure for boutique-retail rebuilds, gallery refreshes, and hospitality renovations where the ceiling is fixed. Standard 1-circuit · 120V · 16A continuous load · UL Listed. Profile visible — 0.85″ tall × 1.30″ wide. Black or White finish to match the head.
