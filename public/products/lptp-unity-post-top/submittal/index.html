One canister housing. Two wattage classes. Two SKUs. Both QuickShip. Unity-I is the cost-engineered cityⒶRCH post-top — a single foldable can-housing that ships in either the 3W75 ECO class (35/50/75W · 115 lm/W · DLC Listed) or the 3W150 high-output class (100/125/150W · 115 lm/W). 3-WATTselect + 3-CCTselect dip-switches field-tune output and color in <30 seconds. Single-can-mount sharing with Symmetry-I across the Unity / Symmetry post-top platform — same hardware, different optical tier. Both active Unity SKUs are QuickShip — LPTP3W75 (ECO class) and LPTP3W150 (high-output class).
