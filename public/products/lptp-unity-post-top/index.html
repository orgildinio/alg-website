Highlights & Features

Unity-I: foldable freight, fixed efficacy, frictionless install.

Seven highlights that drop install cost without dropping light quality — foldable cantilever ships flat, dual 3-step dip-switches turn one SKU into nine settings, Type-V optic puts every lumen down.

— Hero Highlight

Foldable Cantilever Design.

The lamp head folds flat against the pole connector for shipping — a 120° viewing angle that cuts pallet volume by roughly half. Easier to handle on the dock, easier to hoist on-site, and a freight line item small enough that the rep can fold it into the bid.

Field-Adjustable

3-WATTselect × 3-CCTselect.

One SKU, nine in-the-box settings. Set wattage (35/50/75 or 100/125/150) and color temperature (3000K/4000K/5000K) with two dip-switches in under 30 seconds. 115 lm/W platform efficacy holds across every step — no efficacy penalty for trimming output.

Optics

Type V · 360° · Zero Uplight.

Omni-directional pedestrian-scale distribution puts uniform illuminance in a full circle around the pole. The Dark-Sky-aligned cap blocks every lumen of uplight — preserves the night sky, keeps you compliant in IDA-protected zones and most modern lighting ordinances.

Durability

wetⒶGUARD Sealed Housing.

Thermally conductive die-cast aluminum, fully sealed for IP65 wet-location service. Operating range -4°F to 122°F. 3G vibration rating. Dual-mode 6kV surge protection on both L/N-PE and L-N circuits comes standard — 10kV / 20kV available where the spec sheet asks for it.

Mounting

Symmetry-I Single-Can Mount.

Unity-I uses the same Symmetry-I single-can pole adapter as its PRO+ sibling — fits 2-3/8" or 3" round pole tops. One mount, two product tiers. Drops your inventory complexity if you spec mixed-tier projects, and gives you a clean upgrade path from ECO to PRO+ without changing the pole hardware.

Glare Control

Frosted Glass Diffuser.

The frosted glass cap diffuses the LED array into a soft, even disc — eliminates pixelated source spots and the glare that comes with a clear lens. Comfortable for pedestrians looking up, comfortable for security cameras pointing through it, comfortable in residential-adjacent installs.

Energy Optional

Twist-Lock Photocell-Ready.

Standard ANSI twist-lock receptacle in the cap accepts any 3-pin or 7-pin photocell — order with the `/PS` suffix to ship factory-installed, or leave it open and add the LSBX-L1/PCS family on-site. Dusk-to-dawn operation with no controls platform required.
