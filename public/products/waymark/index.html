Highlights & Features

Waymark wins the stairwell + wraparound brief.

Seven highlights anchor the Waymark bid sheet — 32 field configurations from a 2-line catalog, 7-year / 75,000-hour warranty, 125-135 LM/W published efficacy, the largest contrⒶLS + constⒶNT ecosystem in any ALG product, 32 LM-79 IES files, and DampGuard™ standard.

— Bid-Sheet Hammer

32 field configs. 2 catalog SKUs.

What wins the bid. a volume-tier wraparound + WL ship fixed-W + fixed-CCT per part number (1 config per SKU · 16+ part numbers to match Waymark’s coverage). a competing wraparound tops out at 9 configs (3-W × 3-CCT · no 3000K). a category-competitor stairwell fixture same. Waymark gives the spec writer 16 configs per class × 2 sizes = 32 field-set positions across the 2FT and 4FT linear matrix. The contractor sets watt and CCT at install via dip-switch · no return trip to the supply house, no SKU change.

Field Configurability

32 install configs from 2 catalog SKUs.

Two dip-switches on every Waymark driver. 4-WATTselect × 4-CCTselect = 16 configs per class · 32 total across LWRL2 (2FT) and LWRL4 (4FT). a volume-tier wraparound and WL ship fixed-watt + fixed-CCT per part number — spec writers fork 8-12 SKUs to cover the same range. a competing wraparound and a category-competitor stairwell fixture are selectable too, but limited to 3-CCT (NO 3000K warm option for hospitality / multi-family). The contractor sets W + CCT on the job site without re-stocking the fixture.

Lifecycle

7-year / 75,000-hour warranty. Industry-leading.

a volume-tier wraparound = 5 yr / 50k hrs. a volume-tier wraparound = 5 yr. a competing wraparound = 5 yr. a category-competitor stairwell fixture = 5 yr. Waymark is 7 years · 75,000 hours L70. The longest warranty in the volume-spec stairwell-wraparound category. Stairwell fixtures are off-cycle for facility-management maintenance — spec writers pay for warranty length up-front to avoid re-bid traffic from FM 3 years in.

Class-Leading Efficacy

135 LM/W at the 4-foot spec position.

a volume-tier wraparound = 73-115 LM/W. a volume-tier wraparound = 110 LM/W. a category-competitor stairwell fixture = 135 LM/W (parity at full output). a competing wraparound peaks at ~155 LM/W only at the low-output position (5,272 lm @ 33.9W). Waymark wins on efficacy AT the higher absolute lumen output — 6,750 lm on LWRL4 at the spec-grade 50W output where the competing brand caps at 5,272 lm. Lower per-fixture wattage = bigger DLC rebate stack on multi-fixture installs.

Plus the snap-in sensor mount installs without tools — a labor-cost differentiator on 10+ fixtures-per-stairwell installs.

contrⒶLS

Total Adaptive Control. Three platforms. One fixture.

Every Waymark ships contrⒶLS-Ready with the largest sensor ecosystem in the ALG portfolio — 18 line items across three Bluetooth Mesh platforms: Bi-Level, ⒶCS, and SILVAIR. Snap-in sensor mount installs without tools on every fixture.

contrⒶLS Bi-Level sensors provide reliable, cost-effective occupancy detection with automatic high/low power switching. MSM-BLS (Microwave) + MSP-BLS (PIR) · 10-15Vdc · 13FT max mount · IR Remote commissioning (RM-06S · 45FT range · 2x AAA) · UL Listed · IP20 indoor · daylight harvesting via preset lux level. The cost-effective choice for stairwells, corridors, and parking garages that don't require networked control complexity.
