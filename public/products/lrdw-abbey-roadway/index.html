LRDW · Abbey PRO

Spec-grade roadway lighting, simplified—photocell-ready out of the box.

Highlights & Features

Abbey: one SKU on the shelf, four wattages on the pole.

Five reasons Abbey wins the spec sheet — Abbey-II WATTSELECT, 182 lm/W peak efficacy, 3G-vibration coastal build, Title 24 §140.7 PIR-ready, and full TAA + ANSI C136.10 federal compliance.

— Hero Highlight

4-Step WATTSELECT.

Abbey-II ships with a 4-position WATTSELECT switch on every housing — LRDW100 (40/60/80/100W), LRDW200 (80/120/160/200W), LRDW300 (120/180/240/300W). Tune the lumen package to the pole height and IES target without changing the SKU on the truck.

Efficacy

182 lm/W Peak Efficacy.

Top-of-class lumen-per-watt on the LRDW200-A2 at the 80W cell — 14,560 lm at 80W. Beats Lithonia DSX1-LED's typical 130-145 lm/W and Cooper Navion's 158 lm/W on the same Type-III distribution head-to-head.

Durability

Coastal-Grade Build.

thermalⒶRMOR die-cast aluminum housing dissipates 300W-class loads passively. wetⒶGUARD IP66 sealing on the optical chamber. ANSI C136.31 3G vibration rating. -40°F to +122°F operating range. Coastal powder coat finish.

Compliance

Title 24 §140.7 Ready.

Field-installable PIR motion sensor satisfies California Title 24 Part 6 §140.7 Outdoor Lighting Controls. Bi-level dim-on-no-occupancy with adjustable cut-off and hold time. Pairs with the 3-pin or 7-pin photocell for full dusk-to-dawn astronomical scheduling.

Compliance

TAA + ANSI C136.10.

TAA Compliant build for federal, state, and municipal procurement. Vendor-neutral 3-pin (ANSI C136.10) or 7-pin twist-lock photocontrol receptacle accepts any compatible photocell or NEMA-DIM controller. DLC Listed; California JA8 ready. 10-year limited warranty.

Adaptive Controls

Photocell-led. PIR-ready. 0-10V dimmable.

Abbey ships with a vendor-neutral 3-pin (ANSI C136.10) twist-lock photocell receptacle on every housing. Add a field-installable PIR sensor for California Title 24 §140.7 occupancy compliance. Drive bi-level scenes with a 0-10V class-2 input — no gateway, no commissioning, no app required.

01

Photocell-Ready.

3-pin (ANSI C136.10) twist-lock receptacle on the housing top — standard on every Abbey-I and Abbey-II SKU. Optional 7-pin (ANSI C136.41) for NEMA-DIM controllers. Pair with any compatible photocontrol — ALG, Acuity, Lumewave, Echelon — vendor-neutral by design.

  • 3-pin (ANSI C136.10) — standard
  • 7-pin (ANSI C136.41) — optional MTO
  • Twist-lock retention · IP66 sealed
02

PIR Motion Sensor.

Field-installable bi-level PIR — full output on occupancy, dimmed standby on vacancy. Adjustable cut-off and hold time tune the schedule to roadway, pathway, or pedestrian use cases. Mounts to the housing underside and ships ready to wire.

  • Title 24 §140.7 compliant
  • ASHRAE 90.1-2016 occupancy reduction
  • 8-12 ft mounting height · 360° detect
03

0-10V Class-2 Dimming.

Driver supports smooth 10%-100% dimming on a 0-10V class-2 control input. Source-or-sink compatible. Pair with any compliant relay panel, networked controller, wallbox dimmer, or DALI-to-0-10V gateway. No wireless mesh required.

  • 10%-100% dim range · flicker-free
  • Source-or-sink class-2 input
  • Compatible with any 0-10V controller

No mesh, no gateway, no commissioning app. Abbey's controls surface is intentionally minimal — the photocell, the sensor, and the dimming line are the same tools your installer already carries on the truck.
