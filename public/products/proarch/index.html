contrⒶLS

Total Adaptive Control.

proⒶRCH's DLC Dual-Listed status means a single fixture qualifies under both rebate envelopes — and the same fixture supports the controls strategy each envelope demands. Ambient envelope: Title 24 Part 6 § 130.1 networked lighting controls (NLC) via the /SR port — ⒶCS or SILVAIR Bluetooth mesh, both DLC NLC qualified. Low-Bay envelope: ASHRAE 90.1 § 9.4 occupancy / bi-level requirements via Bi-Level standalone — no commissioning, no gateway, drop-in code compliance. One /SR port, three commissioning paths, both rebate categories covered.

Two-stage hardwired bi-level control — full output at occupancy, dimmed standby at vacancy. No commissioning, no gateway, no app. Drop-in compliance for ASHRAE 90.1 § 9.4 and Title 24 Part 6 § 130.1 occupancy reduction requirements. PIR (MSP-BLS) or Microwave (MSM-BLS) · 13ft fixture-mount.
