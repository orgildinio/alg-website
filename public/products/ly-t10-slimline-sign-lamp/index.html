Highlights & Features

The slimline T10 LED retrofit that actually fits the sign cabinet you already have.

Seven highlights anchored to one authenticity — true single-pin slimline T10, not a T8-with-adapter. Three lengths · 100 lm/W constant · no mercury · ETL US/C · 5-year warranty.

— Hero Highlight

The Actual T10. Not a T8 with Adapters.

Most "T10 LED replacements" on the market are T8 lamps with adapter sleeves or R17d/HO double-ended lamps for legacy fixtures. LY-T10 ships the actual slimline T10 single-pin form factor that matches the historical sign-fixture socket. Drops directly in. No adapter sleeves to lose, no ballast bypass orchestration, no housing modification. The lamp the sign cabinet was originally designed for — just LED.

3-SKU Lineup

One Family. Every Sign Cabinet.

4ft / 6ft / 8ft. Three discrete lengths covering 100% of the historical municipal sign-cabinet matrix. Specifiers know what to bid; distributors know what to stock; municipal procurement teams write one PO. No 50-SKU sprawl, no length-by-length supplier hunt, no special-order weeks.

Spec-Line Consistency

100 lm/W. Every Length.

3,200 lm at 32W · 3,800 lm at 38W · 4,400 lm at 44W. The math is identical: 100 lumens per watt across all three SKUs. No length-driven efficacy fall-off, no spec sheet caveats, no engineering judgment about which size delivers the best lm/W. Spec-line consistency a procurement team can trust on every project.

Maintenance Math

5-Year Payback for the Typical Fleet.

Original fluorescent T10 slimline = ~10,000 hours and 60-100W. LED retrofit at 50,000 hours and 32-44W collapses the operational cost. For a typical 500-sign municipal fleet, the math works out to a 5-year payback — just from energy + lamp replacement labor. Bucket-truck rolls drop by 4×. Annual replacement-lamp budget drops by 80%.

Phaseout-Compliant

No Mercury. ETL US/C Cross-Border.

Fluorescent T10 slimline contains mercury — requires hazmat disposal, falls under state-level fluorescent-phaseout mandates (CA · WA · NY · CT and growing). LY-T10 is RoHS-compliant with zero mercury. ETL US/C cross-border listing means cross-border DOT projects don't need separate Canadian listing or paperwork.

Three Base Options

Every Legacy Socket Type.

Single-Pin Slimline (default · the standard slimline sign-cabinet socket) · Med BiPin (legacy T8/T12 fixtures) · HO double-ended (high-output sign cabinets). Three base options cover virtually every fluorescent sign-fixture socket type that's been deployed in the last 50 years. Order the right base for the cabinet you have.

12 Years of Dusk-to-Dawn

5-Year Warranty · 50,000-Hour Life.

50,000 hours = 12+ years at typical 11-hour nightly dusk-to-dawn burn cycles. 5-year warranty backstops it. Most fluorescent T10 peers offered 1-2 year warranties. Most commodity LED retrofit peers offer 3-year warranties. Five years is the right honest number for the form factor.

Engineering · Anatomy

Six details that make this the slimline T10 spec engineers actually want.

Single-pin slimline base on one end. Linear T10 tube body at 1.25" diameter. LED array along the lamp length under a clear lens. Universal voltage input on the contact end. ETL US/C label for cross-border DOT projects. Six engineered details delivered for one job: drop straight into the existing slimline fluorescent T10 socket on the existing voltage with no modification.
