HIGHLIGHTS & FEATURES

Five reasons Symmetry rewires the post-top spec.

cityⒶRCH PRO pedestrian post-top — three field-set dials, five mounts, four contrⒶLS platforms on one housing. Beats Lithonia, Cooper, Lumec, Sternberg, RAB.

— Hero Highlight

Type III, IV, or V. Pick at install.

BeamLogic™ via OpticⒶDAPT — a 3-position dial inside the driver compartment that swaps the fixture between Type III, Type IV, and Type V distribution at install. Walk one housing onto a parking-lot pole, a streetscape spine, or a courtyard plaza and dial in the right pattern on the pole. No direct 90–160W LED post-top peer — Lithonia DPT D, Cooper Belmont, Lumec MetroScape, Holophane GranVille, Sternberg Omega, RAB ALED 5S, Kim Archetype — ships a field-adjustable distribution dial. Every peer locks distribution at the optic SKU.

BeamLogic™ · 197 lm/W

197 lm/W peak. T-IV @ 4000K. 36W.

197 lm/W at the BeamLogic T-IV @ 4000K @ 36W operating point on the LPTP090 housing — the peak-efficacy operating point on the datasheet (page 4). Outpaces the typical 90–160W LED post-top peer band of ~85–140 lm/W. The same OpticⒶDAPT dial that swaps distribution also flattens the lumen / wattage curve so the 36W setting gives up almost nothing on output.

4-WATTSELECT × 3-CCTSELECT × T3/T4/T5

36 configs. Three housings. One field-set call.

4-WATTSELECT (100% / 80% / 60% / 40% of housing max) × 3-CCTSELECT (3000K / 4000K / 5000K) × 3-position BeamLogic distribution = 36 configs per housing · 108 total across LPTP090 (36–90W) / LPTP130 (52–130W) / LPTP160 (64–160W). Dial in wattage, CCT, and distribution at install — one stocked SKU covers three rebate brackets and three optic types.

5 Mount Types · One Housing

Pole. Conduit. Gooseneck. Surface. Wall.

Five mount patterns from the same housing — Pole Mount (QuickShip), Conduit Pendant, Gooseneck, Surface Mount, Wall Mount (MTO). Lithonia DPT D is post-top only. Cooper Belmont is 3″ tenon pole-top only. Lumec MetroScape is post-top only. Holophane GranVille is post-top + retrofit kit only. Symmetry covers 5 install patterns from one stocked housing — mixed streetscape projects spec one fixture instead of three.

contrⒶLS-Ready · 4 Platforms

Four controls platforms. One housing. SILVAIR-neutral.

Four contrⒶLS platforms on one housing: /MSP-BLS · /MSP-BLS-5D stand-alone bi-level (IR-remote commissioned), /MSP-ACS ⒶCS Bluetooth Mesh + cloud SaaS (DLC NLC), /MSP-NLC SILVAIR SIG Bluetooth Mesh (vendor-neutral, gateway-free). Acuity locks you into nLight. Cooper locks you into WaveLinx. Symmetry ships the SILVAIR-neutral upgrade path on the same chassis as the proprietary stack.

contrⒶLS

Three platforms. One housing. SILVAIR-neutral.

Symmetry ships 4 contrⒶLS ordering codes across 3 platforms on one housing. Bi-Level for stand-alone PIR. by ⒶCS for Bluetooth Mesh + cloud — DLC NLC qualified. by SILVAIR for SIG-qualified, gateway-free enterprise mesh. Lithonia DPT D locks you into nLight AIR. Cooper Belmont locks you into WaveLinx. Symmetry ships the SILVAIR-neutral upgrade path on the same chassis.

Two PIR bi-level variants — IR-remote commissioned, no gateway, no IT, no third-party sensor BOM line. /MSP-BLS standard auto high/low switching · /MSP-BLS-5D adds 5 manual dials for site-tuned thresholds. Both: PIR · 49-ft mount · 10-15Vdc · IP65 · UL · pole-mount. Commissioning via optional RM-06S IR remote (45-ft range · 2× AAA · IP20). Drives Title 24 §140.7 / IECC §C405.2 / ASHRAE 90.1 bi-level credits without networking. Daylight-harvest capable: adjust lux threshold (>50 lux), preset standby, 3× press to enable. Spec when bi-level occupancy is the entire ask.
