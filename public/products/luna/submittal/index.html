LUXA Luna is the planoⒶRCH PRO+ specification-grade architectural linear pendant — one platform spec'd across four mounting types and four interchangeable optics, so the architect picks the look on the floor and the SKU stays the same. The VersaⒶRCH 4-way mount (pendant / surface / wall / recessed) lands the same fixture into open ceilings, hard ceilings, walls, and trimless flush recesses. opticⒶDAPT swaps Diffuser / Louver / Micro Reflector / Regressed in the field without re-spec'ing, and the directⒶIM 7-way DIP picks the up/down split anywhere from 100% down through 50/50 to 100% up. Up to 190 LM/W at the Diffuser optic with CRI > 90 and UGR < 22 — and continuⒶRUN seamless joiner spans 1,000W per 277V circuit through a seven-shape joiner family (Straight / L / T / X / Y / V / Z).
