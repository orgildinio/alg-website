Highlights & Features

Luna wins the bid sheet on every published spec.

Seven highlights anchor the Luna spec — 190 LM/W class-leading efficacy, VersaⒶRCH 4-way mounting, opticⒶDAPT four interchangeable optics, directⒶIM 7-way distribution, CRI >90 and UGR <22, and continuⒶRUN 1,000 W with the L/T/X/Y/V/Z joiner family.

— Hero Highlight

190 LM/W. Best-in-class. Period.

Up to 1,200 lumens per foot at 190 LM/W max efficacy on the Diffuser optic. 35% higher than Finelite Series 19 (140 LM/W). 46% higher than competing ECO brands Linta (130 LM/W). 2.4× higher than competing ECO brands BOA baseline (78 LM/W). The published-efficacy leader in the architectural-linear category — the single most-quoted spec on the Luna bid sheet.

Mount Versatility

VersaⒶRCH 4-way mount.

Pendant. Surface. Wall. Recessed (with trimless or flanged trim). All four installation types from one SKU platform. Finelite Series 19 ships pendant-only. competing ECO brands Linta is pendant-primarily. competing ECO brands BOA covers three mounts. Luna is the only volume-tier architectural linear with all four.

Field-Interchangeable

opticⒶDAPT 4 optics.

Diffuser for max efficacy. Louver for UGR <22 office grids. Micro Reflector for accent beam shaping. Regressed for trimless architectural flush. Field-swap any of the four without changing the SKU. Finelite has fixed proprietary optics. competing ECO brands fixed PC diffuser. competing ECO brands 1-2 lens options. Luna leads on optical flexibility.

Distribution

directⒶIM 7-Way DIP.

Seven discrete up/down ratios — 100% down, 90/10, 70/30, 50/50, 30/70, 10/90, 100% up. Open-plan office at 70/30. Hospitality at 50/50. Healthcare corridor at 100% down. Luna gives seven steps. competing ECO brands gives three. competing ECO brands gives two. Finelite ships fixed. The finest distribution control in the spec-grade category.

Spec-Grade Quality

CRI >90 · UGR <22.

Both numbers. Both standard. Both published on the cover. CRI >90 across all three CCTs (3000K · 3500K · 4000K). UGR <22 with the Louver or Regressed optic. Finelite charges premium for 90 CRI option. competing ECO brands doesn't publish CRI. competing ECO brands sits at 84-86. Neither competitor publishes UGR. Luna puts both numbers on the bid sheet.

Architectural Flexibility

continuⒶRUN · 6 joiner shapes.

L · T · X · Y · V · Z. Six geometries, up to 1,000 W per circuit at 277 V. Y-joiners for 120° atrium triples. V-joiners for variable-angle architectural transitions. Z-joiners for offset corridors. competing ECO brands Linta tops at three shapes (L/T/X) at 960 W. competing ECO brands BOA caps at 600 W with no joiner family. Luna delivers the richest joiner geometry in the spec-grade category.
