Field-Tunable · 15 Configurations Per Fixture

Set the watt. Pick the color. Stock one SKU.

Three WATTselect positions × five CCTselect positions on every Solstice fixture = 15 lighting outputs from one SKU. The contractor sets watt and color on-site with two DIP switches — no SKU change, no return trip to the supply house. Volume-tier competitors split CCT range across multiple SKUs (volume-tier downlights) or fix the wattage at one value (volume-tier downlights); Solstice gives the spec writer all 15 from a single line item.

The two dials sit on the back of the housing — accessible during install before the trim ring goes on. Set them once on the job site; the fixture remembers the setting for the life of the install.

Highlights & Features

proⒶRCH-T: the industrial-grade center-basket troffer.

Six highlights you'll feel from bid sheet to commissioning — DLC Dual-Listed rebate stack, 6,500 lm T2 peak, 1 kV surgeⒶRMOR standard, TAA Made-in-Mexico, 45 field configs, and thermalⒶRMOR -20°C cold-start.

— Bid-Sheet Hammer

DLC Dual-Listed Ambient + Low-Bay.

One SKU class qualifies under both DLC utility rebate programs. Stop forking BOMs into a corridor + low-bay track — proⒶRCH covers both lanes from one part number. Max the $/fixture rebate stack on a single bid sheet.

High Output

T2 hits 6,500 lm at 125 LM/W.

The T2 HIGH-OUTPUT class on 2×4 delivers 6,500 lm at 50W — one fixture covers the ground-plane footcandles that take two units at the 12-25 ft low-bay mount. DLC Low-Bay rebate qualifies on T2 output.

Surge

1 kV surgeⒶRMOR. Standard.

IEC/EN 61000-4-5 1 kV surge protection baked into every MXP1 / MXP2 driver — no price-up suffix. The volume-spec answer for warehouses with forklift, compressor, and welder spikes hitting the panel.

Procurement

TAA Compliant. Baked into MXP1/MXP2.

Made in Mexico ships TAA-eligible by default — covers GSA leases, USPS retrofits, DoD installs under the Trade Agreements Act. One proⒶRCH bid sheet covers commercial + federal procurement lanes.

Warranty

5-year / 50,000-hour warranty.

5-yr · 50,000-hr L70 · DLC Premium baseline. Extended 10-yr available on the same SKU class — one part number, two warranty horizons.

Configurable

45 field configs. 5 SKU classes.

T1 STANDARD on 1×4 / 2×2 / 2×4 plus T2 HIGH-OUTPUT on 2×2 / 2×4 — 45 field-set positions from 5 base SKUs. Contractor sets watt + CCT via dip-switch at install. One platform, one accessory ecosystem, one bid sheet.

Cold-Start

thermalⒶRMOR -20°C to 40°C.

-20°C cold-start standard on MXP1 + MXP2 — the answer for cold warehouses, parking garages, and mezzanine retrofits where typical center-basket troffers quit at 0°C.

contrⒶLS

Total Adaptive Control.

One /SR port, three commissioning paths — Bi-Level standalone for drop-in code compliance, ⒶCS Bluetooth mesh, or SILVAIR enterprise mesh. Both DLC rebate envelopes covered from a single fixture.

Two-stage hardwired bi-level — full output on occupancy, dimmed standby on vacancy. No commissioning, no gateway, no app. PIR (MSP-BLS) or Microwave (MSM-BLS) · 13′ fixture-mount.
