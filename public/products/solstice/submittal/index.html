Tri-Mode
TRIAC + ELV + 0-10V dimming from ONE SKU · CRI > 90 truⒶCOLOR · 5-CCTselect · 100° wide beam · WetGuard™ frosted PC diffuser · Wet Location · IC-Rated
Solstice is the planoⒶRCH ECO can-less commercial downlight that compresses three dimming protocols into a single SKU — TRIAC + ELV + 0-10V all drive the same fixture, so the spec writer doesn't fork the SKU when the project mixes architectural phase-cut zones with 0-10V control-circuit zones. CRI > 90 truⒶCOLOR at every CCT (2700K through 5000K, 5-DIP selectable) keeps color rendition consistent as the designer tunes warm hospitality areas against cool task-area zones on the same job. Four aperture sizes (4″ / 6″ / 8″ / 10″) with per-size 3-WATTselect cover 600–3,876 lm at up to 110 LM/W family-best on the 8″ — the same color science, same dimming envelope, same Wet Location / IC-Rated listing across every size on a building. The frosted PC WetGuard™ diffuser delivers a glare-free 100° wide-beam wash that works in office, school, hallway, retail, hospitality, and healthcare ceilings without spec gymnastics.
