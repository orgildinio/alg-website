Highlights & Features

Solstice: built for the bid sheet.

Seven highlights you'll feel from the cut-list to commissioning — family-best efficacy, tri-mode dimming, 90+ CRI, dual wet/IC envelope, tool-free spring clips, 4-aperture family, and full contrⒶLS by ⒶCS support.

— Hero Highlight

110 LM/W on the 8-inch.

3,300 lm at 30W on the LCDL8 — the family-best efficacy across LCDL4/6/8/10. Every 3-WATTselect tier (17W → 1,870 lm · 24W → 2,640 lm · 30W → 3,300 lm) holds the 110 LM/W mark. Published-efficacy leader in the volume-tier can-less category.

Triple Threat

Tri-Mode Dimming. One SKU.

TRIAC + ELV + 0-10V from a single fixture. Smooth flicker-free 100% → 10% dimming on every protocol. Named-dimmer compatibility across the major premium-tier wireless control, Leviton, and Legrand product lines. Solstice handles whatever the GC pre-wired — no return trip to the supply house.

Color Rendering

truⒶCOLOR™ · 90+ CRI standard.

CRI > 90 across all five CCT selections. Tight LED binning keeps unit-to-unit consistency on the install — no warm/cool drift across an open-office grid. Color shift ≤ 180K over the 5-yr warranty per ANSI C78.377-2017. Spec-grade color quality at the volume-tier price.

Location Envelope

WetGuard™ + IC-Rated.

Wet-location rated and IC-Rated for direct insulation contact — one SKU covers bathrooms, kitchens, covered exterior soffits, indoor pool decks, and low-ceiling retrofits. Operating range -4°F to 113°F (-20°C to 45°C). Per UL spec; install to NEC and Local Codes.

Easy Install

Spring-loaded clips. Tool-free.

Spring-action housing clips secure the fixture to the ceiling on first push — no separate hardware, no torque spec. 21" conduit whip standard on every size. Boring-hole template included in the carton for all four apertures. Optional NCP plate for joist installs and JCB/NCP plate with integrated J-Box for T-grid drop ceilings.

SKU Consolidation

4 sizes. One SKU family.

LCDL4 · LCDL6 · LCDL8 · LCDL10 — apertures 4.33" / 6.30" / 8.07" / 8.98" — under one product brand and one SKU encoder. Four apertures from one ordering family — fewer SKUs at the distributor, one warranty, one ⒶCS-compatible accessory line.
