    Fire-Resistance & Listings (2-Hour Rated)
    UL 263Fire Tests of Building Construction and Materials — 2-hour rating when installed per fire-rated ceiling assembly instructions · housing engineered to maintain assembly integrity through the rated burn period without compromising the ceiling membrane
    ASTM E119Standard Test Methods for Fire Tests of Building Construction and Materials · pairs with UL 263 to certify the 2-hour rating · authority-having-jurisdiction (AHJ) submittal acceptance across IBC § 711, NFPA 5000, and most state codes
    ASTM E283Standard Test Method for Determining Rate of Air Leakage — SafeZone housing is airtight listed · qualifies for ASHRAE 90.1 envelope leakage compliance and CA Title-24 air-leakage credit
    ApplicationApproved for multi-family corridors, exit-path ceilings, stairwell soffits, unit-to-unit fire-rated assemblies, and any spec requiring 2-hour fire-resistant ceiling penetrations. No fire-box accessory required — the SafeZone disk + J-Box themselves carry the UL 263 / ASTM E119 listing.
