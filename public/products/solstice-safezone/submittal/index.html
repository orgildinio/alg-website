2-Hour Fire-Resistant · UL 263 + ASTM E119 · thermalⒶRMOR steel housing · CRI > 90 truⒶCOLOR · 5-CCTselect · Wet Location · IC-Rated
Solstice SafeZone is the planoⒶRCH PRO 2-hour fire-resistant commercial downlight — tested to UL 263 and ASTM E119 so the spec writer doesn't need a separate fire-rated enclosure or hood when penetrating a 2-hr rated assembly. The thermalⒶRMOR fire-rated steel housing is IC-Rated and Wet Location listed, so one catalog SKU covers shower rooms, corridors, patient rooms, and any code-driven application where penetration of a rated ceiling would otherwise demand a custom assembly. CRI > 90 truⒶCOLOR at every CCT (2700K through 5000K, 5-DIP selectable) keeps color rendition consistent as the architect tunes between warm public-area and cool task-area zones on the same job. Four aperture sizes (4″ / 6″ / 8″ / 10″) with per-size 3-WATTselect deliver 1000-4000 lm at up to 114 LM/W at the 10″ family-best — the same fire rating, same color science, same dimming envelope across every size on a building.
