Lighting works best when the fixture is selected for the conditions it will actually serve. A hospitality environment and an industrial warehouse can both need LED lighting, but the priorities are different: ambiance and visual comfort in one, durable high-output performance in the other.

## Hospitality: Hotels, Restaurants, and Cafés

Vintage Décor and Nostalgic Décor are designed for spaces where the lamp contributes to the guest experience. Their warm decorative character can support hotel lobbies, restaurant dining rooms, private event spaces, cafés, and lounge seating areas.

The product decision still starts with the exact lamp, base, dimming requirement, and fixture in the project. A warm color temperature and decorative form may be appropriate for one hospitality space while the same setting is not appropriate for every area of the property.

## Industrial: Warehouses, Manufacturing, and Big-Box Retail

The Icarus round high-bay is built for larger, demanding environments such as warehouses, manufacturing facilities, storage areas, and big-box retail. Its high-bay form factor, field-adjustable settings, environmental protection, and controls readiness support a different kind of project conversation.

Mounting height, target light level, operating hours, environmental exposure, and controls requirements should guide the final selection. A field-adjustable fixture provides a defined range; it does not replace a lighting plan or a project-specific review.

## Start with the Environment

The practical comparison is not hospitality versus industrial as a marketing label. It is a question of how the space will be used. Confirm the application first, then use current data sheets and project information to narrow the product and configuration.
