Titan is ALG's PRO-tier linear high-bay platform for demanding industrial applications. The product announcement highlights DLC Premium coverage across the lineup, a seven-year, 75,000-hour warranty, and field-adjustable selection tools intended to reduce stocking complexity.

## Titan's Place in the High-Bay Lineup

Titan is a linear form factor for warehouse, manufacturing, and large retail applications. Tiny addresses smaller linear high-bay opportunities, while Icarus and Jupiter offer round high-bay form factors when the project calls for a different housing geometry or beam approach.

The right comparison starts with the project, not just the fixture shape. Mounting height, target light level, available power, controls, environmental conditions, and the preferred maintenance plan all belong in the selection process.

## Field-Adjustable Performance

Titan combines WATTselect and CCTselect for supported field settings. The product announcement also describes three field-swappable PROOPTICS beam options: 40°, 65°, and 90°. That flexibility can help a contractor or distributor serve different project zones from a defined platform, provided the selected setting still follows the approved lighting plan.

## Built for High-Bay Applications

Warehouse and storage facilities, industrial spaces, and retail interiors may share high ceilings without sharing the same operating conditions. Titan is designed to support those conversations with a high-output platform, damp-rated construction, and controls-ready compatibility.
