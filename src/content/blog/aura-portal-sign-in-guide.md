AURA is Archipelago Lighting's command center for sales representatives. It gives an agency one place to check live orders and tracking, review current pricing and stock, manage customer information, and access commission statements.

## What Is AURA?

AURA extends ALG's direct-to-channel infrastructure for independent sales agencies. Instead of waiting on a callback for a price check, stock update, or commission statement, an authorized rep can work from one current dashboard.

That matters when a rep is managing multiple manufacturer lines and active customer accounts. Pricing and availability conversations often happen in the field, not only at a desk. AURA is designed to make the relevant account and product information available without a separate password to remember.

## What You Will Find Inside AURA

The sales dashboard provides year-to-date performance, quota tracking, top customers, and momentum indicators. Live pricing and stock information supports current conversations, including incoming shipment context where it is available.

The customer portfolio view keeps customer lists, transactions, quotes, invoices, and shipments together. Monthly commission statements include line-level detail and PDF export so a rep can review how a commission figure was calculated.

## How to Sign In

1. Request a sign-in link with the work email address on file with ALG.
2. Look for an email from **AURA · ALG Unified Rep Access**. Check spam or promotions the first time if it does not arrive promptly.
3. Open the message and select **Sign in to AURA**. The link expires after 15 minutes.

The portal is available on desktop, tablet, and phone. If you need help confirming the email address on file, contact your ALG representative.

## A Practical Field Tool

The purpose of AURA is practical: let a rep confirm information when it is needed for a customer conversation. It is not a substitute for project-specific documentation, but it can reduce the time between a question and the right current reference point.
