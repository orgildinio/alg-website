Some projects cannot wait on a standard lead time. This availability update highlighted Vintage Décor, Nostalgic Décor, and the Icarus round high-bay for immediate-shipment conversations from ALG's U.S. warehouse network. Availability changes, so confirm the current SKU and quantity before ordering.

## Vintage Décor: Filament Character, LED Efficiency

Vintage Décor brings decorative lamp shapes together with modern LED performance and smooth dimming. The collection includes globe, candelabra, and tubular forms with warm amber glass and a brass-toned base. Depending on the selected lamp, the platform reaches up to 3.5W, 350 lumens, and CRI up to 92.

For hospitality settings, the Belgravia Victorian model supports a warm 2200K character with 90-plus CRI and dimming down to five percent. That makes it suited to environments where visual comfort and atmosphere are central to the design.

## Nostalgic Décor: Familiar Forms for Contemporary Controls

Nostalgic Décor uses clear glass and steel-base construction across a range of decorative lamp shapes. The collection is designed for spaces where the lamp itself is part of the visual composition while dimming performance and long rated life remain important.

## Icarus: Round High-Bay Performance

For warehouse and industrial projects, the Icarus round high-bay is designed for 20- to 40-foot mounting heights. Its field-adjustable platform combines multiple wattage and color-temperature settings with IP65 protection and controls-ready compatibility.

The point of a stocked, field-adjustable platform is practical range, not a reason to skip project review. Confirm the proposed setting, mounting height, controls, and current documentation for the exact SKU before a project is released.

## Confirm Before Release

Stock status, product configurations, and delivery timing can change. The right next step is a current check with the local ALG representative before the order is placed.
